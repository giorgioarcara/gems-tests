The project

GEMS is an open-science project for brief cognitive assessment. It started with the paper-and-pencil GEMS (2022) and grew into a family of tools for in-person, telephone and web-based screening. The work is a collaboration between the University of Padua and IRCCS San Camillo Hospital in Venice, within the NICLA group.

Open by design

All materials are on the Open Science Framework (OSF) under a Creative Commons licence: record forms, instructions, scoring sheets, analysis code and Shiny apps for the cut-offs.

  • GEMS: osf.io/4t5a8
  • Tele-GEMS: osf.io/t3bma
  • Auto-GEMS: osf.io/fq8g7

Translations and adaptations

Open materials make it easy to translate GEMS and collect normative data in other countries. Each test on this site has one page per language, marked by a flag. New languages are added as translations become available.

If you would like to translate, adapt or norm GEMS in your language, contact us. We are happy to support cross-cultural validation projects.
